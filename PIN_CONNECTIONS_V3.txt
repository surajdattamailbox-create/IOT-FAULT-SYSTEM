Power:
12V adapter + -> correctly rated DC fuse -> main switch -> test circuit positive.
Adapter negative -> circuit return. LM2596 input across fused/switched supply as planned;
adjust LM2596 output to 5.0V and verify with multimeter before connecting ESP32 VIN/5V.
Do not feed 12V into 5V/VIN. Confirm the exact ESP32 board power-input marking.

Sensor current path:
Supply positive -> ACS712 IP+; ACS712 IP- -> load positive. The sensor is in series.
Voltage sensor 1 is across the input supply; sensor 2 is across the load/output.
Observe sensor polarity and module labels. Keep the whole demonstration isolated 12V DC.

Physical rocker switches are power/load-path switches, not GPIO inputs. Do not short the supply.
